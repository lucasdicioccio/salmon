About

Salmon is written by Lucas DiCioccio, who also writes about it, and about the other tools it grows alongside, on his blog at dicioccio.fr. The source is on GitHub.

This site is itself generated by a salmon binary, salmon-docs-sync, which rebuilds the pages with Kitchen Sink from the repository’s resources/ and specs/ directories.

Sibling sites

  • Kitchen Sink — the static-site generator this site is made with, and whose documentation layout the guides and specs use.
  • tramaj — the templating language of Kitchen Sink’s sections.
  • agents-exe — a runner for LLM agents.
  • acme-not-a-joke — the ACME client behind salmon’s certificate-signing recipes (salmon-ops-recipes-experimental).
